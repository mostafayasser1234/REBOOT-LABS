03 // MAKER LAB FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CLICK ANY QUESTION TO EXPAND
01. Do I need prior soldering or electronics experience?
Not at all! Every bench session includes a 10-minute safety and soldering crash course led by a student mentor. We start you on practice circuit boards before you touch your own device.
02. How much does it cost to use the Reboot Labs repair bench?
Using our tools, soldering irons, multimeters, and screwdrivers is 100% free for students. Common components like capacitors, rubber belts, and fuses are also free from our salvaged parts bin.
03. Can I donate broken 1980s or 1990s electronics to the lab?
Yes, please! Even if an old console, VCR, camera, or keyboard is completely dead, we harvest working microchips, switches, and screws to save dozens of other machines. Drop donations at Room 204.
